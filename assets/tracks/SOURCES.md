# Real circuit outline sources

Verified 2026-10-30. These diagrams identify public venue configurations. They do not claim that a BMW training session uses the full circuit; the organiser confirms the actual training area.

## Chengdu Tianfu International Circuit

Asset: `chengdu-tianfu.svg`.

Full 3.265 km configuration shown in the 2026 TCR World Tour Chengdu circuit map (19 turns, anti-clockwise). The display vector is now a single closed centreline with a uniform stroke, derived from the source ribbon's medial line. Pixel-scale brush texture is smoothed and the small T13 opening is joined; the source's circuit shape, hairpins and orientation are retained. This replaces the earlier variable-width filled ribbon and separate patch, which left the upper-left section faint and visibly uneven at small sizes. Event labels, map grid and other artwork are omitted. This is a display diagram, not a survey drawing.

Official source: https://www.fiatcrworldtour.com/images/2026/Circuits_new/Maps/FIA-TCR-WT_2026_Circuit_CHINA_1.pdf

Venue identification and event data: https://motorsport.hyundai.com/media-center/news/2026/tcr-world-tour/11-preview-round-6-chengdu

This is Chengdu Tianfu, not the older Chengdu Goldenport circuit.

## Shanghai International Circuit

Asset: `shanghai-international.svg`.

Grand Prix configuration, 5.451 km. The full main path `path3612` and its original transform are retained from the vector map by Will Pittenger (2009). Alternate configurations, pit lane, labels and markings are omitted; a single monochrome stroke is applied. The geometry was visually checked against the 2026 official F1/FIA maps. Orientation may differ between official maps, but the physical layout is the same.

Vector author: Will Pittenger. Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shanghai_International_Racing_Circuit_track_map.svg

Official verification: https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/china/circuit

FIA official circuit map: https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/2026_chinese_grand_prix_-_competition_notes_-_circuit_map_pit_lane_drawing_emergency_exits_map_battery_containment_area_and_red_zone.pdf

## Tianjin V1 International Circuit

Asset: `tianjin-v1-full.png`, unchanged official transparent PNG, 2041 × 1296.

The current official site labels this full configuration 4.29 km, 19 turns, clockwise. It also publishes a separate 2.4 km, 14-turn short configuration. This frontend uses the full configuration for venue identification. Historical 3.72 km E layouts and simulator-specific configurations were not substituted.

Official venue page: https://www.v1aw.com.cn/circuit

Exact full-layout image: https://user-assets.sxlcdn.com/images/71446/ForJvK4P1QFkJeU7T60rkS2owxND.png

Short-layout reference, not currently displayed: https://user-assets.sxlcdn.com/images/71446/FnlNjHES_DQ4_UU1HP5FUeAy3yvK.png

The PNG is used as an alpha mask to match the interface's monochrome line colour. Its pixels and geometry are unchanged.
